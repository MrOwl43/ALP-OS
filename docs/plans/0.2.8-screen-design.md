# 0.2.8 native screen: look and accessibility

The first native pages (alp-screen) were plain libadwaita grey. This pass brings back the
0.2.7 console's look on the native screen and sets the accessibility rules. All of it lives in
`crates/alp-screen/src/style.rs` (CSS and shared widgets) and `topbar.rs`.

## Look (pass 3, owner feedback 2026-11-25: more modern, like the 0.2.6 console)

Pass 3 keeps pass 2's layout and changes the surface: a deep blue wallpaper; home and sign-in sit
right on it (white words, frosted glass tiles, a white status disc, a white main button and glass
buttons); windows are white with 22 px corners and a soft deep shadow, no frame; lists 16 px,
buttons 12-14 px, fields 12 px with a 2 px border that turns blue in use; page-heading icons are
soft tints with a coloured glyph (list icons stay filled); notes have a coloured stripe; the
version tag is a cream pill; the slider has a blue fill and a round white knob.

### Pass 2 (calmer, like 2016-2020 desktop computers), still the base



- A wallpaper behind everything (soft blue gradient now); light windows with dark text float
  over it with a soft shadow. Inspiration only: no other system's images, icons or names.
- Filled rounded colour icons with a white glyph on tiles, rows and page headings (Adwaita
  symbolic icons, package `adwaita-icon-theme`, added to the installed image with
  `librsvg2-common`).
- Rounded-rectangle buttons: one blue main button, white secondary buttons, red only for
  erasing or switching off.
- Sign-in looks like a login screen: large clock, a round picture, the name, the status, and the
  password field with an arrow button, straight on the wallpaper.
- First-start pages are one window each, like a setup assistant: step dots, a big colour icon,
  centred title and lead line, then left-aligned fields and notes.
- Settings and install are light windows with grouped white lists; install buttons sit in a bar
  along the window's bottom.
- The 15 recovery words sit on a pale yellow note sheet: three columns of five, read down each
  column, each word numbered, large monospace type; the "shown only now" warning under it.

### Themes (later)

Colours and the wallpaper are one `style::Theme` (accent colour + CSS background). A later version
can add colour sets and let the user pick a picture of their own as the background or screen
saver (`url("file:///...")`); nothing else needs to change. Only `Theme::DEFAULT` exists now.

## Top bar (every page, as 0.2.7 T18/T57)

Left: Home (on Settings and Photos), the page name, the alpha/beta tag. Right: network icon with
the address phones use, backup disk state, battery, clock, overall status, Settings, power.
State comes from `model::bar` (unit-tested). Power asks first (Cancel / Restart / Switch off);
it is hidden while disks are being written (install progress, archive being made).

## Reopening an old archive (plan 0.2.8-reopen-archive)

- Install page: old archive disks get their own group, "Archives from before", with a tick box
  and a green disk icon; never greyed out, never erased. Ticking one keeps new archive disks
  out (and the other way round); "Keep running from this USB stick" unticks them. The confirm
  page lists them under "These disks are opened again, not erased".
- First start, step 2: "Type your 15 recovery words" on the same pale sheet as the words were
  shown on, three columns of five read down each column. A space or Enter moves to the next
  word; pasting the whole code into one field spreads it out. The extra passphrase field
  appears only when its tick box is on. A typo marks that word in red; wrong words say that
  nothing changed. The words stay on screen until the archive opens, so one typo does not mean
  typing all 15 again.

## Lock screen and settings (owner, 2026-11-25)

- The lock screen is where the computer spends most of its time: the time small at the top, the
  status disc and words large in the middle with the four facts (temperature, disks, phones,
  last backup) in a 2 x 2 grid of glass tiles, and the sign-in along the bottom in one glass bar.
  Later: settings for when the screen locks and when it goes to the screen saver or turns off
  (5 minutes by default).
- Settings are in categories as in the 0.2.6 console: a list on the left with colour icons
  (Phones, Time & Language, Power, About, Advanced; Advanced only while running from a stick),
  the chosen category's page on the right.

## Disk kinds (owner, 2026-11-25)

Every disk row starts with its kind as a badge: SSD, HDD, USB or Card (alpd's `kind`; the screen
guesses USB and SSD from the transport when an older alpd sends none). The archive is best kept on
a hard disk: HDD rows in "Disks for the archive" carry "Recommended"; SSD, USB and card rows say in
amber why they are not recommended for keeping files for many years. When no archive disk is
ticked and the system disk is an SSD or USB, a warning says the archive would be kept there.
Later (not 0.2.8): SSD and USB disks as a second backup holding the snapshots.

## Accessibility rules

- Dark text on light windows, contrast at least 4.5:1 (grey text #5f5f66 or darker); white
  text only on the darkened top bar and the sign-in page.
- A colour never stands alone: every status has an icon and words.
- Buttons and fields at least 44 px high; base text 12 pt.
- Keyboard focus ring: 3 px yellow, visible on blue and on white.
- Icon-only controls and status icons have a spoken name and the same tooltip.
- Fields have a visible label above them that is also their spoken name; each page puts the
  keyboard in its first field.
- Every page scrolls when the screen is small (1366 x 768 laptops).
